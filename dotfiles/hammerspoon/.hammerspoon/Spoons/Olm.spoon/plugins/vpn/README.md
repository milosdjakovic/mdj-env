# Vpn

Picks a Mullvad relay and connects to it. One flat list merges the connect or
disconnect action with the location search, the action naming where the tunnel
is or where it would go, and every city follows below it ordered by the one you
most recently chose. Typing filters the cities, and once anything is typed the
action row drops out so the top match connects rather than the tunnel toggling.

Opens on Hyper and P, appears in the launcher under Network, and can be searched
without leaving the launcher by typing v or vpn.

While the list is open, j and k move, i confirms the highlighted row, and x
closes it.

Without the backend app installed, the list opens to one row explaining that,
rather than doing nothing.
