# Emoji

A picker over emoji and a wide slice of native Unicode symbols, matched by name,
shortcode, tag, or category, so a word like happy or money or a group word like
food finds the right glyph without knowing its exact name. Selecting a row
inserts the glyph into whatever field was focused before the picker opened, and
emoji always rank above plain symbols in the results. It remembers what you pick,
so an empty field leads with your most used glyphs.

Opens on Hyper and J, appears in the launcher under Tools, and can usually be
searched without leaving the launcher by typing e or emoji, provided the backend
in use owns a list of its own to hand over.

While the list is open, j and k move, i inserts the highlighted glyph, and x
closes it.
