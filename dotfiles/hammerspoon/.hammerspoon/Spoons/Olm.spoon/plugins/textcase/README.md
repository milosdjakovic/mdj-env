# TextCase

Recases whatever text is currently selected. Each row is titled in the case it
offers and previews your own selection rendered that way, so you see the real
result rather than a generic example, and choosing one pastes it back over the
selection in place. The cases you have applied before lead the list, most
recently applied first, and the rest follow in catalog order.

Reached from the launcher, with no dedicated chord, either by its row or by its
alias, tc or case, and a space, which lists every case in the launcher itself.
That list previews your selection when the app you are in reports it through
accessibility, and otherwise previews a sample phrase and reads the selection
when you pick. The launcher row itself says when nothing is selected in the app
the launcher opened over, so the one precondition is visible before opening, and
opening anyway shows the same guidance as a single row.

While the list is open, j and k move, i applies the highlighted case, and x
closes it.
